# New Shopify Store Build — Client Questionnaire

This questionnaire helps us build your site right the first time. It should take about 20–30 minutes to complete. Answer as briefly or in as much detail as you like — bullet points are totally fine.

## Before You Fill This Out

Two quick things first:

1. **Shopify collaborator access** — please add us as a collaborator in your Shopify admin (Settings → Users → Add collaborator). This lets us review your catalog, product structure, and existing media directly so you don't need to send us any exports.

2. **Visual assets** — share a link to a Google Drive or Dropbox folder with any brand assets, product photos, lifestyle images, and video you'd like us to use. If your assets are already uploaded to your Shopify media library, just let us know and we'll work from there.
