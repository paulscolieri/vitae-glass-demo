1. Concentrates collection

Your request: a collection for concentrates (The ARC Mouthpiece, Aura Banger Set, Aura Reclaim).

A new Concentrates collection holds The ARC Mouthpiece, the Aura Banger Set and the Aura Dab Reclaim Catcher, and it is wired in exactly like the other categories: a Concentrates tab on Shop All (between Ashcatchers and Bundles) and a Concentrates entry in the Shop menu.

How to add or remove products yourself

The Shop All tab and the Shop menu both read from the collection, so changing the collection is all you need to do. Nothing in the theme editor has to change.

  1. In Shopify admin, click Products in the left sidebar, then Collections.
  2. Click Concentrates.
  3. In the Products section of the collection page, click Browse, tick the products you want to add, and click Add. You can also type a product name in the search box there.
  4. To remove a product, click the x next to it in that same list.
  5. Click Save (top right). The Shop All tab and the collection page update straight away.

Good candidates that are not in it yet: the "Aura Banger DAB Set" (a different product from the Aura Banger Set), the Dab Conversion Kit, and the three dab bundles.

2. Star rating beside the price

Your request: bring the star rating next to the price on the product page.

The product page now shows the star rating and review count right after the price, on the same line. It uses the same review data as the product cards, links down to the reviews, and hides itself on products that have no reviews yet so there is never an empty row of grey stars.

How to turn it off or on

  1. Online Store, Edit theme on the draft theme, page selector to Products, Default product.
  2. In the left sidebar open Product information and click the Price block.
  3. Tick or untick Show star rating beside the price, then Save.

3. Product video placement

Your request: bring the product video up, under the Buy it now button or below the shipping and tree callout bar.

The product video on each page is embedded inside the product description, which put it far down the column in a small bordered box. It is now lifted out of the description into its own slot directly below the callout bar, sized to the full width of the column, and the description keeps the rest of its text. This happens automatically on every product whose description contains a video, and products without one show nothing extra.

How to move it

  1. Online Store, Edit theme, page selector to Products, Default product.
  2. In the left sidebar under Product information, drag the Product video block up or down (for example directly under Buy buttons), then Save.

4. Mobile: overlapping words in a review card

Your feedback: on mobile there is a weird word overlap.

On phones, a longer review's "Read more" link was landing on top of the "Share" control. The bottom row of each review card (Share, Was this helpful) now flows underneath the text instead of being pinned to the bottom edge, so nothing can collide however long the review is.

5. Rectangle where a square should be

Your feedback: there is a rectangle box where there should be a square.

This was the "Compatible with this piece" row on the product page: each product sat in a tall rounded frame with a small square image floating in the middle. The frames are now square and hug the image, on desktop and phone.

6. Sorting beside the filters, sorting first

Your request: could the sorting be on the same side as the filter and price, with Sorting first.

The collection bar now reads Sort by, then Filter (Availability, Price), grouped on the left. The product count keeps the right edge.

7. Pre-order as a diagonal ribbon

Your request: move the Pre-order sign and make it a diagonal (45 degree) ribbon in the top right.

Wherever the Pre-order marker appears it is now a 45-degree corner ribbon in the top right: on product cards across the site, and on the main image of the product page. The Pre-order button and the made-to-order note are unchanged. Sale and Sold out badges keep their existing style.

8. Build Your Own button in the header

Your request: give the Build Your Own button in the top bar the rounded corners the other buttons have.

It now uses the same corner radius as every other button on the site, so if the button roundness is ever changed in the theme settings, this one follows.

How to change the roundness yourself

  1. In Shopify admin, click Online Store, then Edit theme on Vitae Glass - New Build (DRAFT).
  2. Click the paint-roller icon in the left rail (Theme settings), then Buttons.
  3. Drag the Corner radius slider. 0 is square, 16 is the current setting, and higher values round the ends off further. Every button on the site, including this one, updates as you drag.
  4. Click Save (top right).

9. "Need some inspiration" images cut off

Your feedback: on the Build Your Own page the images of the Need some inspiration cards are cut off at the top on all four.

The card images were being cropped to fill a square. They now fit inside the square, so the full height of each set shows, mouthpiece included.
