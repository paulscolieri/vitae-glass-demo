Shows on any page, once per visit

As agreed: show the age gate on whatever page someone lands on, once per visit.

Until now the age gate only appeared on the homepage, so anyone arriving straight on a product page from an ad or a Google search skipped it. It now appears on whichever page a visitor lands on first.

Once a visitor answers yes, it stays out of the way on every other page and in new tabs for the rest of the visit. It only asks again after the browser is closed and reopened.

A refinement from our side: the "Earn Free Gifts" tab, the rewards button and other popups used to float on top of the age gate. They now wait until the visitor has answered.

How to edit it yourself

  1. Online Store, Customize, then in the left sidebar under Header open Site-wide blocks and click Age gate.
  2. The question, buttons, colors and logo are the same settings as before.
  3. New: Remember the answer is set to For this visit. Switch it to For a number of hours to bring back the old timed behavior.
  4. Click Save.
