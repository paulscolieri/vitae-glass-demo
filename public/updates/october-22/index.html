1. Menu text white at the top of the page

Your feedback: the menu text is white above the fold and only turns dark after scrolling.

On phones, opening the menu at the very top of the homepage showed white links on the light menu panel, which made them close to invisible. The menu now always uses the dark text of its panel, whether you have scrolled or not. This was not caused by a colour setting, so nothing needs changing in your theme colours.

A refinement from our side: the "Earn Free Gifts" tab used to float on top of the open menu and the cart. It now steps aside while either one is open.

2. Review stars on the best sellers

Your request: add review stars to the best sellers section on the homepage.

Every product in "Our most-loved builds" now shows its yellow review stars and review count right next to the price. The numbers come from your reviews app, so they always match the reviews on each product page. Products with no reviews yet simply show the price.

3. Footer too tall, especially on mobile

Your request: the footer takes up too much space, especially on mobile. Reduce the line spacing and the dead space.

The spacing between links, headings and sections is tighter everywhere. On phones the Shop links now sit in a left column, with Company and Help stacked beside them on the right, instead of three long lists one after another. The phone footer is about 40% shorter, the desktop footer is slightly tighter (about 10% shorter), and every link and line of text is still there.

4. Specs and What's included

Your request: relink the "specs" and "included" fields on the product pages.

The "Specs & dimensions" tab now shows each product's own Specs field, in place of the placeholder sentence it had before. A new "What's included" tab, right above it, shows each product's Included field. If a product has nothing filled in for one of them, that tab is hidden on its page, so a customer never opens an empty tab.

How to fill in or edit these yourself

  1. In Shopify admin, click Products and open the product.
  2. Scroll down to the Metafields section near the bottom of the product page.
  3. Edit the Specs or Included field.
  4. Click Save. The tab appears on that product as soon as the field has content, and disappears again if you empty it.

5. White text hard to read

Your question: a customer said the white text is hard to read. Is that a setting in the theme colours?

It was not a colour setting. The homepage banner has a soft dark shading layer behind its white heading, text and menu, but it was not showing, so the white text sat directly on the light parts of the video. The shading now shows, slightly darker on the text side and under the menu, so the text stays readable on every frame of the video. You can also make it lighter or darker yourself (steps below).

How to adjust the shading yourself

  1. Once this update is published, in Shopify admin click Online Store, then Customize on your live theme.
  2. With the Home page showing, click the Vitae video hero section in the left sidebar.
  3. Drag the Text contrast shading slider. 45% is the new default, higher is darker, and 0 turns it off.
  4. Click Save.

6. Floating badges

Your request: stack the free gift badge and the rewards badge on the right using little space, and align and minimize the Spin to Win popup and the $10 off SMS tab on the left.

Right side

The rewards button (the small gift box) used to sit on its own in the bottom right corner. It is now docked directly under the "Earn Free Gifts" tab, at the same width and in the same black, so the two read as one slim strip on the edge of the screen. Both still open exactly what they did before.

Left side: adjustable in your popup apps

The "Spin to Win" tab belongs to your email popup app (Alia) and appears after a visitor closes the popup. Its position, size and style are set inside Alia itself, so you can line it up and shrink it there directly, without any theme changes. The same goes for the "$10 off" tab, which is controlled by the app that shows it. If the apps' settings cannot get them where you want them, see Next phase at the bottom.

7. Mobile page speed

Your question: the speed score is around 85 on desktop and 30 to 40 on mobile. Did the age gate affect it, and are there other ways to speed up mobile?

The age gate is not the problem. We measured it on its own and it adds almost nothing. The two real causes on phones were the homepage banner, which made a phone download a large 2 MB video before it could show anything, and the number of apps loading on every page. We fixed everything on the theme side. The apps are your call, and our recommendations are in the Open / Next phase section at the bottom.

What we changed

  • Banner shows sooner: phones now get a small still image first (32 KB), cropped for a phone screen. The video loads afterwards and fades in over it, and phones get their own lighter version of it.
  • Banner loads smoothly: on a slow connection the banner used to draw itself in bands from the top down. It now shows a soft blurred preview straight away, and the sharp image fades in over it once it has fully arrived.
  • Lower videos wait their turn: the two videos further down the homepage now only load when you scroll near them.
  • Fonts load once: the heading and button fonts were being downloaded twice, and one unused font was being loaded on every page.
  • Unused app code removed: the sales pop-up app was loading three extra code libraries on every page while showing nothing (its notifications are switched off). It is turned off now and can be switched back on any time under App embeds.
  • Age gate: its logo was downloading at four times the size it displays. It now loads at the right size.

What is slowing it down now: apps

Around 30 apps and tracking tools add their own code to every page, and on a phone they all compete with your content for the connection. Those apps install themselves outside the theme, so only removing them (or changing their settings) makes a difference. Our recommendations are in the Open / Next phase section below.

Which apps do you still use?

Age gate on every page? The age gate you added only appears on the homepage. A visitor who lands straight on a product page (from an ad or a Google search, for example) never sees it. If it should cover every page, let us know and we will move it.
