1. Concentrates collection

Your request: a collection for concentrates (The ARC Mouthpiece, Aura Banger Set, Aura Reclaim, ARC Mouthpiece).
There is no Concentrates collection yet. Confirming the exact product list and where it should appear (a Concentrates tab on Shop All and an entry in the Shop menu is the assumption) before it is created.

2. Star rating beside the price

Your request: bring the star rating next to the price on the product page.

Today the product page shows the title, price and shipping note with no rating; the stars only appear on product cards and in the reviews section further down.

3. Tolstoy video placement

Your request: bring the Tolstoy video up, under the Buy it now button or below the shipping and tree callout bar.

4. Mobile: overlapping words in a review card

Your feedback: on mobile there is a weird word overlap.

Reproduced: on a phone, a longer review's "Read more" link sits on top of the "Share" control at the bottom of the card. It only happens on reviews long enough to be trimmed.

5. Rectangle where a square should be

Your feedback: there is a rectangle box where there should be a square.

This is the "Compatible with this piece" row on the product page: each product sits in a tall rounded frame with a square image floating in the middle of it, on both desktop and phone.

6. Sorting beside the filters, sorting first

Your request: could the sorting be on the same side as the filter and price, with Sorting first.

Today the Filter controls sit on the left and Sort by on the far right of the bar.

7. Pre-order as a diagonal ribbon

Your request: move the Pre-order sign and make it a diagonal (45 degree) ribbon in the top right.

Today Pre-order is a rounded badge in the bottom-left corner of the product card image, the same spot as the Sale and Sold out badges.

Assumed to apply to product cards; confirming whether the product page image should carry the same ribbon.

8. Build Your Own button in the header

Your request: give the Build Your Own button in the top bar the rounded corners the other buttons have.

Today it is the only square-cornered button on the site.

9. "Need some inspiration" images cut off

Your feedback: on the Build Your Own page the images of the Need some inspiration cards are cut off at the top on all four.

The card images are forced into a square and cropped to fill it, so the tallest part of each set (the mouthpiece) is trimmed off the top.
