1. Post-build upsell discounts

Your request: pair the add-ons offered after a build with a discount, with the percentage set by you, per product.

When a customer adds a build to the cart, the "Your build is in" prompt offers featured add-ons. Each featured add-on can now carry its own discount tier: 10%, 15%, 20% or 25% off, or none, chosen from a dropdown in the theme editor. The prompt shows the original price crossed out, the reduced price, and a percentage badge, so the offer is clear before the customer decides. When they click Add, the discount is applied to the cart automatically: the customer never types a code, and the cart and checkout show the reduced price.

Behind the scenes each tier is a real Shopify discount code with a random suffix, so it cannot be guessed, and it is never advertised anywhere on the site. The codes only discount products in a dedicated "Configurator upsells" collection (hidden from the storefront), so the offer cannot leak onto bundles or other products. The four codes are already set up in your store and work with your other promotions.

How to set the discount on an add-on

  1. In Shopify admin, click Online Store in the left sidebar (under Sales channels).
  2. Under Draft themes, find Vitae Glass - New Build (DRAFT) and click Edit theme.
  3. Click the page selector at the top center of the editor (it reads Home page), choose Pages, then Build Your Own.
  4. In the left sidebar, click the Vitae configurator section to expand it. Its blocks are listed underneath: the four preset cards, then the Featured upsell blocks (currently Swiss Freeze Mod and Aura Banger DAB Set).
  5. Click a Featured upsell block. The settings panel on the right shows Product and, directly under it, the Discount dropdown: None, 10% off, 15% off, 20% off or 25% off.
  6. Pick a tier and click Save (top right). The prompt updates immediately; nothing else needs to change.

How to add a new discounted add-on

  1. First, in Products, Collections, open Configurator upsells and add the product to it. This collection is hidden from the site; it only tells Shopify which products the discounts may apply to. A product that is not in it would show the reduced price in the prompt but be charged full price at checkout, so always do this step first.
  2. Back in the theme editor (steps 1 to 4 above), click Add block under the Vitae configurator section and choose Featured upsell.
  3. Pick the Product, choose a Discount tier, drag the block to set its order in the prompt, and click Save.

The four discount codes themselves are recorded in the section's "Upsell discount codes" fields. They do not need to be touched; if one is ever deactivated or renamed in admin, paste the new code into the matching field there.

2. Slate & Sand color palette, chooser removed

Your pick: the Slate & Sand palette with the classic header, and the floating palette chooser removed.

The Slate & Sand palette is now the site's real color set, applied everywhere: page backgrounds, buttons, badges, accents and section bands. The floating palette and header chooser is gone. The header keeps the layout you chose (the "Classic" option).

Ivory Light Sand Soft Blue-Grey Deep Slate Blue

How to adjust any color yourself

  1. In Shopify admin, click Online Store in the left sidebar (under Sales channels).
  2. Under Draft themes, find Vitae Glass - New Build (DRAFT) and click Edit theme.
  3. Click the paint-roller icon in the left rail (Theme settings), then Colors.
  4. The site uses five color schemes. Scheme 1 is the page background, Scheme 2 the sand panels, Scheme 3 the blue-grey bands, Scheme 4 the dark slate sections, Scheme 5 the accent buttons. Click a scheme to change its background, text and button colors.
  5. Click Save (top right). The change applies to every page that uses that scheme.

3. Shop All switches categories without reloading the page

Your feedback: jumping between collections on Shop All refreshed the whole page instead of filtering in place, which felt laggy.

Clicking a category tab now swaps the product grid in place. The page no longer flashes white and reloads; the tab underline and product count update, the new products fade in, and the web address still changes so a category can be bookmarked or shared and the browser's back button steps between categories. The Filter and Sort controls keep working on the switched grid.

Both recordings below run on the same deliberately slowed connection so the difference is visible at normal speed, and the orange counter in the corner shows how many times the page has loaded. Before: a tab click is a full page load (blank screen, counter climbs with every click, scroll position lost). After: two category switches, still one page load.

Managing the tabs themselves is unchanged: Online Store, Edit theme, page selector to Collections, Default collection, then the Vitae collection tabs section.

4. "Completes your build" images show without hovering

Your feedback: in the Completes your build section on product pages, the main image only appeared on hover.

The product recommendations under a product load a moment after the rest of the page, and the hover-preview behaviour on cards was only being set up for cards present at first load, so those cards had no visible image until you hovered. Cards that appear after the page loads (recommendations, recently viewed, a switched Shop All grid) now get their image immediately, and the hover preview still cycles through the extra photos.

5. Product page: callout bar under the buy buttons

Your request: callout icons you can configure higher up on the product page, right under the cart buttons (free shipping over $200, free returns, easy to clean, and so on).

A compact icon-and-text bar now sits directly under Add to cart and Buy it now on every product page, in the spot where shoppers are deciding. It ships with four callouts: Free U.S. shipping over $200, 30-day easy returns, Easy to clean, and 1 tree planted per module. Because the same claims apply to every product, the bar is one shared block on the product template, edited in one place rather than product by product. The old four-box strip at the bottom of the product page was showing the same information twice, so it has been removed from product pages (it stays on the homepage and the Build Your Own page).

How to change the callouts yourself

  1. Online Store, then Edit theme on the draft theme.
  2. Click the page selector at the top center, choose Products, then Default product.
  3. In the left sidebar, open the Product information section and click the Icon with text block (it sits just under Buy buttons).
  4. There are four pairings. For each one pick an Icon from the list (truck, return arrow, check mark, plant, star, snowflake, lock and more) or upload your own small Image, and type the Heading.
  5. Leave a pairing's Heading empty to hide that slot (the bar collapses to three or two).
  6. Drag the block up or down in the sidebar to move the bar (for example under the description instead), then click Save.

If a single product ever needs a different claim, that can be added later as a per-product override without redoing this.

6. Benefits strip: divider between the rows on mobile

Your feedback: on mobile, the benefits strip (free shipping, one tree per module, and so on) needed a divider when it wraps to two rows.

On phones the four-item strip wraps to two rows of two. There is now a horizontal rule between the rows and the stray vertical line on the right edge is gone, so it reads as a clean two-by-two grid. This applies wherever the strip is used (homepage, Build Your Own); both images below are the Build Your Own strip on a phone.

7. Reviews: fixed-height cards with a Read more reveal

Your feedback: the review cards were too big, every card stretched to match the largest one instead of a fixed height with a show-more reveal.

The review carousel comes from the Stamped app, and it stretched every card in a row to match the tallest one, so a single long review made all four cards huge with empty space. Review text is now limited to six lines with a Read more link on the reviews that were cut off; clicking it expands that card in place (and Show less collapses it). Each card is only as tall as its own content. The rating summary, photos, sorting, search and Write a review are untouched.

This is styling layered over the Stamped widget. Everything else about reviews (which reviews show, sorting, the questions tab) is still managed in the Stamped app.

8. Why Vitae: tree planting photo

Your feedback: the one-tree section on the Why Vitae page was missing its image.

The "One tree planted per module" section was showing the theme's placeholder graphic. It now uses the One Tree Planted photo already in your store's files (seedlings being planted in Ghana, the same photo set as the current Our Cause page).

How to swap the photo yourself

  1. Online Store, Edit theme, then page selector to Pages, Why Vitae.
  2. In the left sidebar click the Image with text section ("One tree planted per module").
  3. Under Image, click Change to pick another photo from your files or upload a new one. A landscape photo around 1200 by 800 pixels works best.
  4. Click Save.

9. Build animation: longer, more deliberate

A refinement from our side: the complete-build animation went by too quickly to register, so we lengthened it.

When a build is completed, the chosen components fly together into an assembled stack before the summary appears. The pieces now fly in a little more slowly and the assembled stack holds on screen longer before the summary, so it reads as a moment rather than a flash: about 4.4 seconds from click to summary instead of 2.9. Clicking anywhere still skips straight to the summary, and the hold time is adjustable (steps below).

How to tune the length yourself

  1. Online Store, Edit theme, then page selector to Pages, Build Your Own.
  2. Click the Vitae configurator section in the left sidebar.
  3. Find Hold time after assembly (ms) and drag the slider. This is how long the assembled stack stays on screen before the summary; 2350 is the current setting, 1000 is brisk, 4000 is a long hold.
  4. Click Save.

10. Pre-order instead of Sold out

Your request: show Pre-order instead of Sold out when an item is out of stock but can still be ordered.

A product that is out of stock but has Shopify's Continue selling when out of stock turned on now shows a Pre-order button on its page, a short note under the buttons ("This item is made to order. We will email you the estimated restock and ship date after checkout."), and a Pre-order badge and button on its product card. Products that are out of stock without that setting still show Sold out. Nothing changes for products that are in stock. Both states are shown below on the preview theme.

How to control which products show Pre-order

  1. In Shopify admin, open Products and click the product.
  2. Scroll to Inventory. If the product has variants, click a variant first.
  3. Tick Continue selling when out of stock to allow pre-orders for that variant, or untick it to show Sold out instead.
  4. Click Save. The site picks up the change immediately.

Right now 209 variants have this turned on and 276 do not. Which ones should allow pre-orders is a stock decision on your side; nothing on the site needs to change either way.
